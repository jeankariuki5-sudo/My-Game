- Player movement (WASD/arrow), health, animated hit reactions
- Auto-aim, auto-fire guns  up to 6 equipped at once, orbiting the player
- Pooled projectiles that auto-return after 2s or on enemy hit
- Two enemy types: normal chaser, and a "charger" that pauses, then dashes at the player- with support for multiple charger variants 

- Score and materials (currency) earned per kill
- 4 upgrades (Damage, Fire Rate, Speed, Max Health), each with escalating cost - price increases by a set amount every n purchases
- Gun purchasing in the shop; when all 6 slots are full, buying a new gun opens a slot-picker UI so the player chooses which equipped gun to replace, instead of blocking the purchase

- Waves scale in enemy count and spawn rate as you progress
- Wave timer with countdown, wave-cleared vs. wave-failed (timeout) states
- Shop opens automatically between waves
- Tilemap/background swaps on each wave clear

- High score and last-wave-reached saved and shown on the game-over screen
- Game over and restart flow
- Main Menu scene with Start/Quit with navigation between scenes 
- Background music 

- Pause menu (Escape key), cursor lock/unlock, resume/quit
- 1-in-100 chance for a killed enemy to drop a health pickup
